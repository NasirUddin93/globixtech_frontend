Bangladesh's e-commerce sector is booming. But as your business grows, you'll eventually face a critical decision: should you stick with a monthly subscription service (SaaS) or invest in a custom-built solution?

## The SaaS Route (Shopify, Wix, etc.)
**Pros:**
- Quick setup
- Lower initial cost
- Managed hosting and security

**Cons:**
- Monthly recurring fees
- Limited customization
- Transaction fees on every sale

## The Custom Solution (GlobixTech E-commerce)
**Pros:**
- **Zero Monthly Fees:** You own the software and don't pay per sale.
- **Infinite Scalability:** Add any feature you need as your business evolves.
- **Brand Identity:** Complete control over the user experience and design.
- **Local Integration:** Tailored for Bangladeshi payment gateways and logistics.

**Cons:**
- Higher upfront investment
- Requires a maintenance partner

## Which Should You Choose?
If you're just starting out with a few products, a SaaS platform might be the way to go. However, if you're a growing brand processing hundreds of orders daily and want to build long-term value, **Custom is the winner.**

A custom platform is an asset, not an expense. It's the difference between renting a shop and owning the building.
