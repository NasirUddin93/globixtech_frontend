Managing inventory effectively is crucial for retail stores, warehouses, and e-commerce businesses. The right software can streamline your operations, but with so many options, what should you look for?

## 1. Real-Time Tracking

You need to know exactly what you have in stock at any moment. **Real-time tracking** prevents overselling and helps you restock on time.

## 2. Barcode Scanning Support

Manual entry is slow and prone to errors. Ensure your software supports:
- Barcode generation
- QR code scanning
- Mobile app scanning

## 3. Multi-Location Management

If you have more than one store or warehouse, you need a system that can handle transfer of goods between locations seamlessly.

## 4. Reporting & Analytics

Data is power. Look for software that provides:
- Sales reports
- stock aging analysis
- Profit margin calculation

## 5. Low Stock Alerts

Never run out of best-sellers again. Automated alerts for low stock levels ensure you reorder before it's too late.
